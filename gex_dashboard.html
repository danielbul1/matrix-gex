What is the Options Heat Map?

The Options Heat Map shows where call/put premium is concentrated across strike prices and expiration dates. Blue cells show call premium leading, red cells show put premium leading, and brighter cells mark stronger relative activity.

Net Flow (Premium)

Net Flow tracks call premium, put premium, and the underlying reference price over the market session. Spikes can help show where options activity is leaning toward confidence or fear.

Max Pain

Max Pain estimates the strike where option-holder payout is lowest at expiration. Use it as a reference zone, especially when current price is far from the Max Pain strike.
